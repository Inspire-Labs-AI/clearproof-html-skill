Great question! The "port already in use" error that you are seeing is generally caused by the fact that another process, which may possibly be an earlier instance of your own server that was never properly shut down, is still holding on to the port that your application is attempting to bind to, and it is typically a good idea to first try to identify which process it might be before you decide to do anything drastic. In most cases, you will probably want to begin by running a command such as lsof, which should hopefully show you the process that is occupying the port, and then, assuming that the process is not something important, it may be worth considering terminating it so that the port can potentially be freed up. It is also worth noting that, depending on your operating system and setup, the exact commands might vary a little, and in some situations you might also want to simply choose a different port altogether, which could be an easier solution.
